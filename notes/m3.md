# M3 — Disorder and lateness: results

- **Date:** 2026-11-26
- **Data:** the M1 slice (card slice 20:0, 2026-03-01 → 2026-06-15): 115,720 transactions,
  417 cards, 956 frauds, one partition
- **Model:** `models/2e632818a205` (FEATURE_SET_V2), thresholds review 0.0412, block 0.4609
- **How:** `bash scripts/m3_drills.sh`; raw reports in `notes/m3/`
- **Status:** complete. Decision recorded in ADR-011.

## 1. Tooling

| Piece | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| `application/disorder.py` | Seeded arrival order: each event is delayed with probability p (exponential delay, rounded to seconds, capped) and duplicated with probability q |
| `simulate --delayed-fraction ...` | Publishes the arrival order; `verify-state` with the same flags replays the same order |
| `application/watermark.py` | Newest event time seen and the distance of each event behind it |
| `ApplyEvents` | Per partition: `fq_event_lateness_s` (histogram) and `fq_late_events_total` |
| `finspeq-offline lateness-sweep` | Scores each event at arrival (window read before the write) and compares features and decisions with canonical order, once per allowed lateness |

An event is **late** when it is more than the allowed lateness behind the newest event time of
the partition, and **card-late** when it is that far behind the newest time of its own card.
The store prunes per card, so only card-late events can lose entries to pruning.

## 3. Lateness sweep

Mismatched = feature vector differs from the canonical-order vector (exact float equality).
Retention = stored history relative to 24 h.

### Mild

| Allowed lateness | Retention | Late | Card-late | Mismatched | Decision changes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 1.000 | 9,947 | 269 | 289 (0.250%) | 6 |
| 60 s | 1.001 | 8,157 | 220 | 289 | 6 |
| 600 s | 1.007 | 1,340 | 37 | 283 (0.245%) | 6 |
| 1 h | 1.042 | 0 | 0 | 283 | 6 |
| 6 h | 1.250 | 0 | 0 | 283 | 6 |

### Heavy

| Allowed lateness | Retention | Late | Card-late | Mismatched | Decision changes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 1.000 | 22,435 | 4,590 | 7,120 (6.15%) | 46 |
| 60 s | 1.001 | 22,034 | 4,518 | 7,108 | 46 |
| 600 s | 1.007 | 18,859 | 3,861 | 6,948 | 44 |
| 1 h | 1.042 | 7,756 | 1,550 | 6,396 (5.53%) | 43 |
| 6 h | 1.250 | 40 | 3 | 6,047 (5.23%) | 42 |

- **Pruning error** (recoverable with lateness) is the drop from 0 to 6 h: 6 events (mild),
  1,073 events (heavy). One hour recovers all of it under mild disorder and 724 (67%) under
  heavy disorder.
- **Missing predecessors** (not recoverable with lateness) are what remains at 6 h: 283 and
  6,047 events. These events were read before earlier events of the same card had arrived.
- The 1 h features (`tx_count_1h`, `spend_1h`) never change with lateness: 4,046 heavy
  mismatches at every value. Pruning only removes entries more than 24 h behind, which 1 h
  features never read.

Under heavy disorder late arrival costs about 1% of block recall (8–10 frauds); allowed
lateness wins back 2 of them. The flag rate is unaffected from 1 h upward.

## 5. System check: disordered stream through the broker

Heavy disorder published to a fresh one-partition topic, applied by a worker crashing after
apply with p = 0.05 per 50-event batch, then compared with an in-memory replay of the same
arrival order (`verify-state` with the same disorder flags).

| | Lateness 0 | Lateness 1 h |
|---|---|---|
| Published arrivals | 116,864 (1,614/s) | 116,864 (1,008/s) |
| Worker crashes | 109 | 109 |
| Max group-offset lag behind the store | 150 | 150 |
| Window mismatches (417 cards) | **0** | **0** |
| Offsets covered | 116,864 of 116,864 | 116,864 of 116,864 |

Replay equality holds under disorder and duplicates: the final state depends only on the
arrival order, not on batching or crashes. The two publish rates differ only through machine
load; the profiles are identical.

## 6. Decision

Allowed lateness 1 h, late events applied (ADR-011). Deviations from the plan:

- Bounded block shuffling was not built; exponential delays already produce bounded and
  long-tail disorder.
- Memory is reported as the retention factor, not measured in Redis.
- No `transactions.late` side topic.
